Three decades behind the night.

GEM2i's origins date back to an entertainment and promotions partnership first established in 1993. In 2002 it was reorganized to serve different segments of the nightlife and live-music business.

With our own business development methodology we connect promoters, festivals, venues and artists in more than 17 markets across North America, South America, Europe and Asia.

Carlos Artiles
