About us

Three decades behind the night

GEM2i's origins date back to an entertainment and promotions partnership first established in 1993. In 2002 it was reorganized to serve different segments and demographics of the nightlife and live-music business.

With our proprietary business development methodology we connect promoters, festivals, venues and artists in more than 17 markets across North America, South America, Europe and Asia.

Today GEM2i is a private membership network: guest lists, QR e-passes and e-tickets for the best events, festivals and conferences in the world.
