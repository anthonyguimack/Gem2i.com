About the festival

In 1999, the first edition of Ultra Beach Music Festival was hosted on the sands of Collins Park in Miami Beach. Far from the global powerhouse it is today, Ultra was a small gathering of electronic music fans at the apex of the Winter Music Conference.

This Amsterdam festival is the place for the industry to round off the year, but it is much more than a music conference: a city-wide five-day program of talks, showcases and club nights.
